Croatia - published administrative guidance on immigration case handling
Checked 2026-11-15.

Result: Croatia publishes almost no guidance for decision-makers. One real guidance
document was found (the Government's inter-agency Protocol on unaccompanied children,
2018). The other 14 files are the minister's rulebooks (pravilnici) from the official
gazette, i.e. legal acts, kept only because they are the working manual in practice.
  guidance        1 file  (2018)
  procedural act 14 files (2020-2026: 5 base rulebooks and their 9 amending rulebooks)
  Total 15 files, 2.6 MB, dated 2018-2026.

Who decides
- Ministry of the Interior (Ministarstvo unutarnjih poslova, MUP): police administrations
  and police stations decide temporary residence, stay-and-work permits, EU Blue Cards,
  long-term residence and return; the Directorate for Immigration, Citizenship and
  Administrative Affairs decides international protection (first instance), Dublin and
  second-level residence matters.
- Croatian Employment Service (Hrvatski zavod za zapošljavanje, HZZ): labour-market test
  and the opinion on which most stay-and-work permits depend.
- Ministry of Foreign and European Affairs / consulates: visas.

What is in this folder
- guidance: Protokol o postupanju prema djeci bez pratnje (Government, 30 Aug 2018,
  48 pages): step-by-step duties of police, doctors, social welfare and MUP for
  unaccompanied children, including age assessment, the international protection
  procedure, Dublin and return.
- procedural acts (HTML pages of Narodne novine, unmodified, one file per gazette item;
  the gazette has no consolidated texts, so amendments are separate files):
    Pravilnik o boravku državljana trećih zemalja (NN 20/2022, amended 155/2022, 43/2026)
    Pravilnik o postupanju prema državljanima trećih zemalja (NN 136/2021, 145/2023, 102/2025)
    Pravilnik o ulasku i boravku ... državljana EGP (NN 107/2020, 148/2020, 86/2021, 155/2022)
    Pravilnik o boravku u prihvatnom centru za strance (NN 145/2021, 155/2022, 137/2023)
    Pravilnik o vizama (NN 109/2021; Ministry of Foreign and European Affairs)
  Left out on purpose: the statutes; rulebooks on forms and data collections, fees, legal
  aid, means of subsistence, language test, reception conditions and health care; quota
  decisions; the national anti-trafficking protocols (victim identification, not
  immigration decisions).

EU Blue Card / temporary work agencies
- EU Blue Card: only in the residence rulebook (NN 20/2022: 13 mentions; NN 43/2026
  amendment: 26 mentions) - documents to file, proof of higher professional skills through
  the opinion of the commission that assesses experience-based skills, the certificate
  printed on approval, change of employer. No guidance document addresses it.
- Temporary work agencies: two provisions in the NN 43/2026 amendment (the certificate
  issued where the employer is an agency; a seasonal worker may work for the agency and
  the user undertakings named in the certificate, change of user on a positive HZZ
  opinion). Nothing on employers of record. No guidance document addresses either.

How much real guidance: very little. The internal instructions of MUP exist but are not
published anywhere; what an official can be seen to work from is the Aliens Act, the
International and Temporary Protection Act and the rulebooks above, plus EUAA practical
guides in Croatian. The single published guidance document is the 2018 protocol on
unaccompanied children.

Completeness doubts

  menu pages and by web search, so a stray PDF under /UserDocsImages/ could have been
  missed.
- The "uputa" gazette search was read only for the 200 most recent of 1,034 titles.
- Amendments to the rulebooks after April 2026 would not be in the script's list.
